DESCRIPTION (one paragraph)

Persistence of Forms is a real-time generative piece of video and stereo sound for one screen or projection. Spheres, tetrahedra and cubes bounce inside a turning wireframe cube, drawn like an old phosphor screen, and every bounce plays a chime tuned to a soft drone. The shapes never change. What wears out is the piece's memory: everything it plays is fed back through a six-second loop, and a clock that runs for the ten days of a show sets how worn that loop is. The first days are nearly clean. By the end, echoes sink lower and duller, notes drift off key, lines break into stair-steps and red ghosts, and shapes are drawn where they were rather than where they are. The clock is kept on the computer, so a refresh does not heal the piece, and a visitor who comes back on another day meets the same shapes and a different piece. It is built on FORMS, an earlier piece by Checo Cadena from checocadena.com. The software runs and has been tested in compressed runs; the repository records no public showing yet.


SHORT BIO

Checo Cadena is a New York-based singer-songwriter, sound artist and creative technologist from Monterrey, Mexico, who writes and produces records and builds the machines around them: plugins, robots, installations and software. Since 2023 Cadena has been Computer Control Engineer at Amorphic Robot Works, Chico MacMurtrie's robotic sculpture studio. Cadena holds a B.S. in Integrated Design and Media from NYU Tandon and an M.A. in Creative Media and Technology from Berklee NYC.
